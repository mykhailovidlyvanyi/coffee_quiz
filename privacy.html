Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Morning Dial is a coffee quiz demo. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the website and how you can control optional analytics.

1. About Morning Dial

Morning Dial is a coffee quiz demo that helps visitors explore their coffee preferences and receive a personalized coffee profile and recommendations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit and use the Morning Dial website.

2. Information We Process

Quiz responses

Your answers to the quiz questions are used to generate your coffee profile and recommendations. The quiz is designed to calculate and display your result in your browser.

Analytics data

If you accept optional analytics, Morning Dial uses Amplitude to understand how visitors interact with the website and to improve the quiz experience. Depending on your interactions and the enabled analytics features, this may include:

  • Pages and quiz screens viewed
  • Quiz starts, answers, and completion
  • Interactions with results and recommendations
  • Website usage and technical information collected by analytics tools

Session Replay may also be enabled to help understand how visitors interact with the website.

Email field

The email field shown in the demo is for demonstration purposes only. The current version of Morning Dial does not send or store the email address entered into this field.

Consent preference

The website stores your analytics consent choice in your browser's local storage so it can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through the website is used to:

  • Generate your quiz result and coffee recommendations
  • Understand how visitors use the quiz, if optional analytics are enabled
  • Identify usability issues and improve the website

We do not use the demo email field to create mailing lists or send marketing emails.

4. Analytics and Local Storage

Morning Dial uses Amplitude for optional analytics and Session Replay.

Amplitude is not loaded until you accept optional analytics. If you reject analytics, the website should continue to work without starting Amplitude.

The website uses local storage to remember your choice. Analytics technologies may use additional storage or collect technical information once enabled.

You can change your choice by clearing the website's saved data in your browser. Doing so may also remove other locally saved website preferences.

5. Third-Party Services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. If you accept analytics, Amplitude provides analytics and related functionality.

These providers may process technical information as part of delivering their services. Their processing is also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Amplitude Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention and Security

The consent preference is stored locally in your browser until you clear the website's saved data or it is otherwise removed by your browser.

Analytics data, if collected, is handled according to the applicable settings and retention policies of the analytics provider.

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary data collection. However, no website or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can reject optional analytics and still use the quiz.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us using the email address in the Contact section below.

8. Children's Privacy

Morning Dial is a general-audience demo and is not intended to collect personal information from children. Please do not submit personal information through the website.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, with the updated date shown at the top.
